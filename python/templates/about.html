About the Project

An automated system that classifies news text as real or fake using Natural Language Processing and supervised Machine Learning.

Dataset

Kaggle "Fake and Real News Dataset" — True.csv (~21,417 genuine articles) and Fake.csv (~23,481 fabricated articles). Columns: title, text, subject, date. Labels: REAL = 0, FAKE = 1. Split 80% training / 20% testing, stratified, random_state = 42.

Algorithm

Text is cleaned (lowercasing, URL/HTML/punctuation removal, stop-word removal, Porter stemming) and vectorised with TfidfVectorizer(stop_words='english', max_df=0.7). Logistic Regression then models P(fake | x) = sigmoid(w·x + b); a probability of 0.5 or higher is labelled FAKE. Multinomial Naive Bayes and Random Forest are trained for comparison in train_model.py.

Results

Logistic Regression ≈ 96% accuracy, Random Forest ≈ 96%, Multinomial Naive Bayes ≈ 93% on the held-out test set. Confusion matrices and the comparison chart are written to results/ when training runs.
